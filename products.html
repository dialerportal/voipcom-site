Pakistani DID

City numbers your customers trust

A DID is simply a plain phone number — like a Karachi 021 number — that lives in the cloud instead of in a SIM or a landline socket. Your customer sees a familiar city code, so they pick up. And you answer from wherever you are.

Local identity

Get a 021, 042, 051, 041 or 061 number so customers in that city feel a local business is calling — not some unknown mobile number they would ignore.

The number is your company's

This number belongs to your company, not to one employee's SIM. Staff come and go — the number, and every customer who saved it, stays with you.

Rings anywhere

Your Karachi number can ring in the app on a phone in Lahore, Islamabad, or wherever your team sits today. The city code is for the customer — you do the work from anywhere.

Business cloud PBX

Your own exchange, no hardware

A PBX is the same "exchange" big companies use — the system that welcomes the caller, gives them a menu, and connects them to the right person. Ours runs in the cloud, so there is no box on the wall, no wiring, and no engineer visit. You can be live in a single day.

  1. 01

    Pick a number

    Get a new city number from us — or bring your number question to your demo and we will walk you through all your options.

  2. 02

    Record your IVR greeting

    The IVR is that voice menu — "press 1 for sales, press 2 for support." Record it in Urdu, English, or both, in your own voice or ours.

  3. 03

    Add your team as extensions

    An extension is a short internal number for each person — 101 for sales, 102 for support. Everyone logs in from the mobile app. No separate SIM per employee.

  4. 04

    Go live and watch the reports

    Calls start reaching your team through the menu, and you see every answered and missed call in one simple live report.

IVR receptionist, 24/7

This voice menu welcomes every caller professionally — even at 2 in the morning — and sends them to the right person or to voicemail.

Extensions and transfers

Give every team member a short number and pass a call to each other in one tap — no need to tell a customer to "call this other number."

A queue for busy hours

Call recording

Live reports
