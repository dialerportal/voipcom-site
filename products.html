Pakistani DID

City numbers your customers trust

A DID is just an ordinary phone number (a Karachi 021 number, say) that lives in the cloud rather than in a SIM or a landline socket. Your customer sees a city code they recognise, so they pick up. You answer from wherever you happen to be.

Local identity

Take a local number (051 today, more city codes next) and customers in that city see a local business calling, rather than an unknown mobile number they'd let ring out.

The number belongs to the company

The number sits with your company, not on one employee's SIM. Staff will come and go. The number stays with you, and so does every customer who saved it.

Rings anywhere

Your Karachi number can ring the app on a phone in Lahore, Islamabad, or wherever your team is sitting today. The city code is for the customer's benefit. You do the work from anywhere.

City codes: Karachi 021 · Lahore 042 · Islamabad 051 · Faisalabad 041 · Multan 061 (051 Islamabad is live today; 021, 042 and mobile numbers launch next)

Business cloud PBX

Your own exchange, no hardware

A PBX is the same "exchange" the big companies use: the system that greets a caller, offers them a menu and puts them through to the right person. Ours runs in the cloud, so there's no box on the wall, no wiring, and nobody needs to come out and install anything. You can be live within a day.

  1. 01

    Pick a number

    Take a new city number from us, or bring your questions about numbers to the demo and we'll walk you through your options.

  2. 02

    Record your IVR greeting

    The IVR is the voice menu: "press 1 for sales, press 2 for support." Record it in Urdu, English or both, in your own voice or ours.

  3. 03

    Add your team as extensions

    An extension is a short internal number for each person, such as 101 for sales and 102 for support. Everyone logs in from the mobile app, so nobody needs a separate SIM.

  4. 04

    Go live and watch the reports

    Calls start reaching your team through the menu, and you can see every answered and missed call in one simple live report.

IVR receptionist, 24/7

The voice menu greets every caller properly, even at 2 in the morning, and sends them to the right person or to voicemail.

Extensions and transfers

Give each team member a short number and pass calls between you in one tap. Nobody has to tell a customer to "call this other number."

A queue for busy hours

When everyone is on a call, new callers wait in a queue with music rather than hearing a busy tone. The next free person picks up.

Call recording

Every call is saved. You can settle a "who said what" dispute in minutes, and train new staff on real calls.

Live reports

See how many calls were answered, missed and left waiting, by person and by day. You'll finally know what actually happens on your phones.

Your office in your pocket

The mobile app keeps your office number with you. Answer as the business from the shop, the warehouse or your home.

Stop fake COD orders before they ship

  2. 02

  3. 03
