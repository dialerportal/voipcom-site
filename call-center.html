FAQ

What operators ask us.

Can my agents work from home or from different cities?

Yes. Agents log in from a browser or app with a headset. One supervisor dashboard covers agents in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad at the same time.

Which dialer modes do you support?

Predictive, progressive and preview dialing for outbound campaigns, plus inbound queues with skills-based routing and IVR. We pick the mode per campaign on the scoping call.

Is a cloud call center legal in Pakistan?

Yes. Public calls and Pakistani numbers are delivered through PTA-licensed operators; voipcom works with licensed operators. Export call centers should also register with PSEB — we guide you through it.

How long are recordings kept?

90 days by default on the pilot plan; longer retention and export to your own storage are available on floor plans.

How fast can we start?

A pilot team is usually live in 24 hours. Larger floors take a few days to onboard agents, load campaigns and connect your CRM.
