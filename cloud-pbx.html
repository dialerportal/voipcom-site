FAQ

What business owners ask us.

Do I need to buy any hardware for a cloud PBX?

No. The PBX runs in our cloud. Your team answers on the mobile app, a laptop, or an IP desk phone if you already own one. No box on the wall, no wiring, no technician visits.

Can I keep my current business number?

Often, yes. It depends on your current operator and we check it on the demo call. A new 051 (Islamabad) number is provisioned the same day — Karachi 021, Lahore 042 and mobile numbers are launching next, so many businesses run both numbers while they switch.

Does a cloud PBX work on mobile data?

Yes. The app works on 4G and Wi-Fi, so a salesperson in the field or a manager at home answers the office number from their phone, and the customer sees your business number, not a personal SIM.

Are calls recorded, and for how long?

Every call is recorded. Recordings are kept 30 days on Starter and 90 days on Team, searchable and downloadable from your portal. Longer retention is available on Business plans.

What happens to calls if my internet drops?

Calls forward to a mobile number you choose, so customers still reach you. When the connection returns, the app takes over again. Nothing to reset.
