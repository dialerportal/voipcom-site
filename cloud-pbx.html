FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

Do I need to buy any hardware for a cloud PBX?

No. The PBX runs in our cloud. Your team answers on the mobile app, a laptop, or an IP desk phone if you already have one. Nothing gets mounted on the wall, and nobody needs to come out and wire anything.

Can I keep my current business number?

Often, yes. It depends on your current operator, and we check on the demo call. A new 051 (Islamabad) number is provisioned the same day. Karachi 021, Lahore 042 and mobile numbers are launching next, so many businesses run both numbers while they switch over.

Does a cloud PBX work on mobile data?

Yes. The app works on 4G and Wi-Fi, so a salesperson on the road or a manager at home can answer the office number from their own phone. The customer sees your business number, not a personal SIM.

Are calls recorded, and for how long?

Every call is recorded. Recordings are kept for 30 days on Starter and 90 days on Team, and you can search and download them from your portal. Business plans can keep them longer.

What happens to calls if my internet drops?

Calls forward to a mobile number you choose, so customers still get through. When the connection comes back, the app takes over again. You don't need to reset anything.
