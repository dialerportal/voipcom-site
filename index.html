FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to buy any hardware?

No. Everything runs in the cloud. Your team answers from a mobile or desktop app; IP desk phones are optional if you want them on reception.

How fast can we go live?

Most businesses are live within one working day — number provisioned, IVR recorded, extensions added. Larger call centers take a few days to onboard agents and campaigns.

Is this legal in Pakistan?

Yes. Public voice and city numbers in Pakistan are delivered through PTA-licensed operators. voipcom works with licensed operators and is a Pakistan-registered company — nothing grey, nothing offshore.

How is it billed?

Monthly, in PKR, by a Pakistani company. No dollar invoices, no surprise conversions, cancel monthly plans anytime.

Can my AI voice agent use a Pakistani number?

Yes. We provide Pakistani DIDs with SIP delivery that plug into Retell, Vapi, Bland and similar platforms, so your agent can make and receive local calls with a local number. See AI phone numbers →
