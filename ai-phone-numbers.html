FAQ

What developers ask us.

Does this work with Retell, Vapi or Bland?

Yes. All three accept a custom SIP trunk. We give you SIP credentials and the settings for each platform; inbound calls to your Pakistani number reach your agent, and outbound calls from the agent leave with your number as caller ID. Custom Asterisk or FreeSWITCH stacks connect the same way.

Will outbound calls show my Pakistani number as the caller ID?

Yes. Outbound calls are delivered through a licensed operator with your own DID as the caller ID, so a Karachi customer sees a 021 number, not a foreign or masked one. Caller ID can only be set to numbers on your own account.

What KYC documents do I need for a Pakistani DID?

Business registration documents and the ID (CNIC or passport) of the authorised person. Numbers are provisioned through a PTA-licensed operator after KYC, usually within 24–48 hours. We send the exact checklist when you request numbers.

How many concurrent calls can one number handle?

More than one. Each DID comes with multiple concurrent channels, and we size the channel count to your campaign. Tell us your peak simultaneous calls and we quote accordingly.

Where is the SIP edge, and what latency should I expect?

Calls are delivered from a Pakistan-facing SIP edge, so the media path to Pakistani customers stays short. Exact latency and jitter figures depend on where your agent platform is hosted; we share measurements on request and run a test call with you before go-live.
