What an IVR system is

An IVR system in Pakistan is the automated voice menu that answers your business number and lets the caller choose with the phone keypad. IVR stands for Interactive Voice Response — but forget the acronym. The idea is simple: instead of a phone ringing and ringing until someone happens to be free, a recorded voice picks up instantly and says, "Welcome to your company. For sales press 1, for support press 2." The caller taps a key and the call goes straight to the right team.

That greeting answers on the first ring, every single time — at 9am, at midnight, during load shedding, during Eid. Your business sounds bigger, no caller hears a busy tone, and your team stops fielding calls meant for someone else. If you want to see exactly how a call travels from the menu to the right extension, our guide on what a PBX is walks through one full call flow.

The one-line version: an IVR is a receptionist that never sleeps, never takes a break, and always sends the caller to the right place on the first ring.

Urdu and English greetings

Your greeting is recorded in whatever language your customers speak. Record it in Urdu, in English, or in both — a warm welcome in Urdu followed by menu options in English is a common and comfortable choice for Pakistani callers. You can re-record it yourself whenever you like: new Eid hours, a Ramadan schedule, a seasonal sale, or a brand-new department. Change the words, and the change is live in minutes.

A good greeting is short and clear: who you are, then the options. "Assalam-o-alaikum, welcome to Company Name. For sales press 1, for support press 2, to speak to the front desk press 0." That is all it takes to sound like an organised business instead of a mobile number someone answers when they can.

Routing, queues and after-hours

What happens after the caller presses a key is called routing. You decide where each option goes, and you can change it any time from the app. Here is a typical menu for a small Pakistani business:

Caller pressesWhere the call goes
1 — SalesRings the whole sales team as a queue
2 — SupportRings the support extensions in turn
3 — AccountsGoes to the billing extension
0 — Front deskGoes to the operator or reception
No key / after hoursPlays a closed greeting, records voicemail, emails it to you

A few pieces make the routing work in real life:

  • Queues. When every agent on an option is busy, callers wait their turn with hold music instead of a busy tone — and the next free person takes the next caller.
  • After-hours schedule. Set your office hours once. Outside them, the IVR plays a different greeting and sends callers to voicemail or to a mobile you choose.
  • Voicemail-to-email. Any message a caller leaves arrives in your inbox as an audio file, with the number and time — so nothing is missed overnight.

Why every business number needs an IVR system

An IVR system in Pakistan solves the everyday problems that quietly cost you customers. Missed calls stop being lost sales, because the menu answers even when your team cannot. Callers reach the right person without being told to "call this other number." You sound like an established company from the very first second. And because every call runs through the system, you get a clean record of what happened — useful whether you run a clinic, a distributor, a property office, or an online store confirming COD orders.

It also scales without pain. Add a new agent and they simply join the sales queue — no new SIM, no printed number to reissue. The number belongs to your business, not to whoever happens to answer it. IVR system questions, answered

What is an IVR system?

IVR stands for Interactive Voice Response. It is the recorded voice menu that answers your business number and lets the caller choose with the keypad — for example, press 1 for sales, press 2 for support. It answers on the first ring, every time, day or night.

Can I record the IVR greeting in Urdu?

Yes. Record your greeting in Urdu, in English, or in both — a welcome in Urdu and the menu options in English is a common choice. You can re-record it whenever you like, for Eid hours, a sale, or a new department.

Do I need special hardware for an IVR system?

No. The IVR runs in our cloud. There is no PABX box on the wall, no wiring and no engineer visit. Your team answers the routed calls from the mobile or desktop app, so you can be live in a single day.

What is IVR call routing?

Call routing is how the IVR sends each caller to the right place after they press a key. Press 1 can ring the sales queue, press 2 the support extensions, press 0 the front desk. If everyone is busy, the caller waits in a queue with music instead of a busy tone.

Can the IVR forward calls after office hours?

Yes. Set your office hours once and the IVR plays a different after-hours greeting and sends callers to voicemail — emailed to you as an audio file — or forwards to a mobile you choose. No caller is ever left with a dead ring.
