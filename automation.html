FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Which stores work with the COD confirmation calls?

Shopify and WooCommerce plug in directly. Any other store or order panel connects through our API. If a new order can reach us, we can call it, and a cancelled order never goes to the courier.

Does the AI voice agent speak Urdu?

Yes. We set agents up in Urdu, English or both. We record your greetings and write the script with you, and nothing goes live until you've approved it.

What happens if a customer wants to talk to a human?

The agent passes the call to your team as soon as the caller asks, or when it runs into a question its script doesn't cover. Outside your hours it takes a message and logs it to your CRM, so someone on your team can call back in the morning.

How fast can we get automation running?

A COD confirmation flow on Shopify or WooCommerce is usually live within one working day. An AI voice agent takes a few days, because we write, record and test the script with you before it answers a real call. Nothing gets installed at your end.

How much do confirmation calls and AI agents cost?

Confirmation calls are billed per answered call and AI agents per minute, on top of a monthly plan. Extra extensions are priced per extension, with volume pricing for 5+. The exact rate depends on your volume, and we confirm it on the scoping call. Local minutes are billed at cost, in PKR.
