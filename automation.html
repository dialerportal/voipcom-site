FAQ

What owners ask us.

Which stores work with the COD confirmation calls?

Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly. Any other store or order panel connects through our API, so if a new order can reach us, we can call it. Cancelled orders never reach the courier.

Does the AI voice agent speak Urdu?

Yes. Agents are set up in Urdu, English or both. We record your greetings and write the script with you, and you approve it before the agent takes a real call.

What happens if a customer wants to talk to a human?

The agent transfers the call to your team the moment the caller asks, or whenever it hits a question outside its script. Outside your hours it takes a message and logs it to your CRM so your team calls back in the morning.

How fast can we deploy automation?

A COD confirmation flow on Shopify or WooCommerce is usually live within one working day. AI voice agents take a few days because we write, record and test the script with you before it answers a real call. No hardware is installed at your end.

How much do confirmation calls and AI agents cost?

Confirmation calls are billed per answered call and AI agents per minute, on top of a monthly plan; the Team plan is PKR 7,500 per month. Exact rates depend on your volume and are confirmed on the scoping call. Local minutes are billed at cost, in PKR.
